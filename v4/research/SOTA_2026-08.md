# Memory V4 SOTA Research Report — LLM Agent Memory Architectures (August 2026)
_Provenance: Anthropic deep-research run, 2026-08-14, session claude-web-0813e. Primary-source citations inline (arXiv IDs). This is the REQUIRED INPUT for wq #569-578; validate+localize, don't re-survey._

## TL;DR
- The tail-closing wins that fit our constraints are cheap and CPU-viable: entity-match fusion channels (Mem0's April 2026 report attributes its largest gains — +29.6 temporal, +23.1 multi-hop — to fusing semantic+BM25+entity), a tiny CPU cross-encoder reranker (Ettin-17M reranks ~50 candidates in ~187ms fp32 and beats every MiniLM and bge-reranker-v2-m3 on MTEB), and contextual-chunk augmentation for prose (Anthropic's pattern cut top-20 retrieval failure 35/49/67%). Graph DBs, LLM-in-hot-path, and framework adoption correctly SKIPPED at <10K notes.
- V3's governance model (proposal-only, provenance, supersede-not-delete, human-gated instincts) is squarely aligned with 2026 safety literature: SSGM, Governed Collaborative Memory, and GovMem independently converge on human-ratified, evidence-checked promotion. Poisoning literature (MINJA 98.2% injection / 76.8% attack success via queries alone) validates that automated curation is the vulnerability; the human gate is the defense.
- Adopt reranking + entity channels + usage-logging first (highest ROI, CPU-cheap); adapt contextual chunking and a two-counter usage-salience signal; build SHARED as a schema-validated localhost daemon (PatchBoard JSON-Patch validation + Collaborative Memory provenance) — never a graph DB. Build the stratified per-class harness FIRST, because published rerankers demonstrably HURT some hard-query classes.

## Key Findings
1. **Reranking is the highest-leverage tail fix — only tiny models are CPU-viable, and they may not help our hardest classes.** i7-13700K benchmark (HF Ettin blog, May 2026): 267 pairs/s (Ettin-17M) down to 6 pairs/s (bge-reranker-v2-m3, 568M → ~3.3s/20 docs, DISQUALIFIED for p50<400ms). Ettin-17M: ~50 candidates ~187ms, outscores all ms-marco-MiniLM and bge-v2-m3 on MTEB. BUT MultiConIR (arXiv:2503.08046): pointwise cross-encoders degrade sharply on multi-condition queries (Win Rate -39%) — our outage-rca/procedure tails may benefit less than factoids.
2. **Entity-match fusion is the mechanism behind Mem0's biggest gains, and it re-implements what we already have.** Vendor numbers, but mechanism clear+CPU-cheap: semantic + BM25 (lemmatized) + entity-boost fused; no-LLM temporal-intent classifier additively nudges (never filters).
3. **Contextual chunk augmentation is the best-measured prose win.** Anthropic: top-20 failure 5.7%→3.7% (embeddings, -35%), →2.9% (+contextual BM25, -49%), →1.9% (+rerank, -67%). Offline write-path cost, proposal-only-compatible.
4. **Safety literature validates our invariants precisely.** MINJA 98.2%/76.8% query-only; widely-cited 2026 analysis: >90% agents vulnerable, 100% relapse on conversational correction ("only fix is at the data layer"). Human-gated, provenance-tracked, supersede-not-delete = the field's converged defense.
5. **Graph systems (Zep/Graphiti, G-Memory, MAGMA, LiCoMemory) measured but overkill at 1K notes.** Zep's +18.5% LongMemEval requires a graph DB (invariant violation). Our 7,910-edge SQLite table already captures the 1-2 hop value.
6. **Usage-weighted salience has a clean formula now.** "Memory Worth" (arXiv:2604.12007) = two counters (success/failure co-occurrence), provably converging to p+(m) = Pr[success | m retrieved]. Maps directly onto GOVERNED (instinct adherence vs outcomes).
7. **2026 benchmark wave gives transferable classes and a warning.** LongMemEval / MemBench / MemoryAgentBench (ICLR'26) / MemoryArena (ICML'26) / BEAM (10 dims incl. abstention, contradiction, event-ordering) — all conversational; none match ops known-item. Our 193-query golden set is the better instrument for our class.

17M/32M Ettin Pareto-dominate legacy MiniLM (higher quality AND faster); 32M beats bge-v2-m3 (17x larger) on MTEB. fp32 numbers = conservative; ONNX-int8 (officially supported, pre-exported qint8 exists for MiniLM-L6) ~1.5-2x faster.
**Caveat — may not help hard classes:** MultiConIR (2503.08046) reranker Win Rate -39% on multi-condition (2x retriever decline); NVIDIA (2409.07691) MiniLM-L12 LOWERED NDCG@10 0.6100→0.5771 on a strong dense retriever; conversational study (2507.13937) MiniLM-L12 cut MRR 42%. Reasoning-IR wins (BRIGHT 2407.12883; 2509.07163) come from LLM/listwise rerankers — infeasible at CPU p50<400ms. VERDICT: ADOPT gated on per-class golden win; expect factoid gains; measure RCA/procedure before trusting.
**Entity channel:** Mem0 Apr-2026 report + docs: semantic + BM25(lemmatized) + entity, parallel-scored, fused; temporal-intent classifier additive-only. Claims +29.6 temporal / +23.1 multi-hop, LoCoMo 92.5 @ ~6,956 tok/call vs ~26K full-context. VENDOR numbers on managed platform ("proprietary optimizations not in the OSS SDK"), conversational benchmarks. Mechanism sound + free: our edge-extracted entities (tickets/files/subjects/nodes) promoted to an additive non-filtering fusion channel targets entity-heavy RCA. VERDICT: ADOPT.
**HyDE/expansion:** HyDE improves semantic alignment for short queries but needs hot-path LLM (invariant violation). Index-time alternative HyPE (Vake 2025): hypothetical QUESTIONS per doc at index time, up to +42pp precision/+45pp recall, zero query-time cost. VERDICT: ADAPT HyPE offline (dream); SKIP query-time HyDE unless benchmark-justified.
**Graph:** LiCoMemory +9% LoCoMo/+26.6% multi-session; MAGMA multi-graph; G-Memory (NeurIPS'25 spotlight, 2506.07398) three-tier hierarchy for MAS; Zep/Graphiti (2501.13956) +18.5% LongMemEval but graph-DB-required. VERDICT: SKIP graph DBs; keep typed/weighted SQLite edges.
**Fusion:** RRF (1/(k+rank), k≈60) = parameter-free zero-shot default; learned normalized combos preferred when tuning data exists (Bruch 2022) — we have 193 labels. Calibrated fusion (2603.28886): percentile-rank normalization robust for heterogeneous channels. VERDICT: ADAPT — RRF baseline, tune per-channel weights on golden, consider percentile norm.

## RQ2 — FEDERATED/PROSE INDEXING (TOTAL)
**Contextual retrieval (Anthropic):** prepend 50-100 tok LLM-generated context to each chunk before embedding AND BM25. Measured: -35% / -49% / -67% top-20 failure (embeddings / +ctx BM25 / +rerank). Offline write-path. VERDICT: ADOPT for the 473KB operator file + session archives.
**Granularity:** small precise chunks (~200-500 tok, Anthropic ~500) + section-aware boundaries respecting markdown header hierarchy; contextual augmentation restores lost context. Late chunking (2504.19754) = alternative preserving cross-chunk context.
**EDU decomposition (2512.14244 LingoEDU):** clause-level units in a source-anchored tree; requires trained SFT model — heavier than needed. Transferable idea: decision-atom granularity (one gotcha = one retrievable unit) beats paragraph chunks. VERDICT: ADAPT the principle; SKIP the model.
**Dedup:** embed prose chunks, cosine vs existing notes, flag near-dups into dream proposal flow (never auto-suppress). VERDICT: ADOPT contextual chunking + section-aware + dedup-via-proposals.

## RQ3 — MULTI-AGENT MEMORY SERVICES (SHARED)
**PatchBoard (2605.29313):** validated JSON-Patch mutations over shared structured state; Architect defines schema + role write-contracts + runtime invariants; deterministic kernel validates each mutation before transactional commit. Verbatim result: 84.6% success vs 30.8% LangGraph / 61.6% Flock; 45.5k tok/success vs 368.3k / 64.2k (630 ALFWorld episodes, Qwen-plus, temp 0). Cleanest published template for schema-validated writes + per-lane contracts over SQLite. VERDICT: ADAPT.
**Collaborative Memory (2505.18279, Accenture):** private/shared tiers; IMMUTABLE provenance attributes (agents, resources, timestamps) enabling retrospective permission checks; read/write policies as bipartite user-agent-resource graphs with time-evolving access. VERDICT: ADAPT provenance + scoped read/write policies (extend our origin/trust) + audit.
**Also:** 2603.10062 (cache/coherence framing); Governed Shared Memory (2606.24535) + Governed Collaborative Memory (2605.04264): candidates need inspectable evidence + review; explicit ratifying authority; fixation = shared-layer entry with provenance, version lineage, supersede-not-erase — near-exact description of our model. **GovMem (2607.02579)** on real traces: 0/133 safe automatic promotions, 11/11 gate positives rejected — naive auto-promotion over-promotes; correlated traces mislead vote-counting; dependency-aware support scoring needed.
**Failure modes:** Always-On Agents survey (2606.30306): "corrupt or stale state crosses users or agents through the shared substrate"; revocation + propagation control = the under-addressed gap. Cognee flagged for destructive chunk-replace with no peer-reviewed eval. VERDICT: BUILD memoryd = localhost CLI+HTTP, PatchBoard kernel, per-lane provenance, audit + quarantine, supersede-not-delete + EXPLICIT revocation/propagation control.

## RQ4 — USAGE-WEIGHTED SALIENCE + FORGETTING (EARNED)
**Memory Worth (2604.12007):** two counters per memory (success/failure co-occurrence) → converges a.s. to p+(m)=Pr[success|m retrieved] under stationary retrieval; Beta-Bernoulli posterior mean; dual counts encode confidence. Usable for staleness detection, retrieval suppression, deprecation PROPOSALS.
**Taxonomy (2512.13564):** time-decay (MemGPT eviction, soft weight decay) / frequency (XMem LFU, MemOS LRU, KARMA counting Blooms) / importance-composite. Published decay fns: exponential recency x similarity; Weibull (SSGM cites Huang 2025); ACT-R power-law base activation + access reinforcement. Generative Agents: recency+importance+relevance weighted sum.
**Newer:** DeltaMem (2604.01560) RL + Levenshtein-distance reward, residual chains; Darwinian Memory (2601.22528) utility-selection pruning (GUI domain); FadeMem (2601.18642) + FSFM (2604.20300) adaptive differential decay.
**Usage vs recency evidence:** Mem0's own eviction blog concedes forgetting "unsolved at tooling level"; recommends TTL + LRU-decay-on-retrieval + active supersession + SALIENCE FLOOR (the "I told you yesterday" failure). Strongest pro-usage evidence: Memory Worth convergence proof + ACT-R access-reinforcement lit. VERDICT: ADOPT usage logging NOW (zero-risk); ADAPT two-counter signal → additive boost + forgetting PROPOSALS (never auto-delete); salience floor on core tier; fits half_life columns + dream.

## RQ5 — POISONING + GOVERNANCE (GOVERNED)
**Attacks:** MINJA (NeurIPS'25, 2503.03704): "98.2% for injecting malicious records...attack success rate of 76.8%" solely via queries (Webshop/MIMIC-III/MMLU, no privileged access). AgentPoison (NeurIPS'24, 2407.12784): <0.1% poison rate, ≥2 instances → retrieval success 81.2% avg, 59.4% generates target actions, 62.6% actual impact — NOTE: 80%+ is RETRIEVAL success, end-to-end ≈62.6%; benign perf impact 0.74%. PoisonedRAG 91-99% w/ 5 texts in millions. The >90%-vulnerable/100%-relapse pairing traces to "Designing Agentic Memory in 2026" (secondary; "the only fix is at the data layer"). Now OWASP ASI06. Systematic: MPBench (2606.04329).
**Defenses for derived/external paths:** EHR study (2601.05504v2) verbatim: baseline ASR 53%; "With persistent memory (initial few-shot examples plus accumulated queries), ASR drops to 0%"; correct initial memory cut injection 100%→25% — a well-curated store is ITSELF a defense. SSGM (2603.11768): governance middleware decoupling cognition from substrate; constrained retrieval + gated writes + async reconciliation w/ consistency verification, decay, access control pre-consolidation. Runtime screening layers (allow/redact/quarantine/block + SHA-256 tamper baselines) emerging as OWASP tooling.
**Human vs automated curation:** GovMem 0/133; Governed Collaborative Memory: explicit ratifier + evidence + supersede-not-erase. VERDICT: our invariants ARE the recommendation. KEEP proposal-only + human gate; ADD write-path anomaly screening (redact/quarantine on derived/external trust); measure instinct effectiveness via Memory Worth outcome correlation.

## CAVEATS
- Mem0 numbers = vendor content, managed platform ("proprietary optimizations not in OSS SDK"), conversational benchmarks. Use mechanism, distrust magnitudes. Their 2026 rewrite REMOVED the traversable graph (entities now ranking-only) — aligned with our no-graph-DB stance.
- Reranker CPU latencies = fp32/SDPA single-CPU estimates; ONNX-int8 not rigorously benchmarked per-model. Conservative upper bounds; measure on our hardware.
- Small pointwise rerankers may HURT hardest classes (2503.08046; 2409.07691) — the single most important SHARP caveat.
- AgentPoison "80%+" = retrieval-stage, not end-to-end (~62.6% per 2407.12784). Cite correctly.
- Zep independent LongMemEval = 63.8% (vs Mem0 49.0%, GPT-4o, Vectorize eval cited in 2512.13564); Zep self-reports ~71.2 — use the independent number. Graph-DB-required; Community Edition deprecated Apr 2025.
- The 90%/100% poisoning pairing = secondary sourcing; cite MINJA/AgentPoison for hard numbers.
- BEAM/LongMemEval inflatable by lenient LLM judges + context-stuffing — our exact-match set is the more trustworthy instrument.
- Memory Worth assumes stationary retrieval; convergence weakens under distribution shift — recompute periodically, outputs are proposals not truth.
